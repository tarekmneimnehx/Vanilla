Our story

Baked at home, with all our heart

Vanilla Concept began in a small home kitchen with a big love for making moments unforgettable.

How it started

From a passion to a craft

What began as gifts for family and friends grew into a little dessert studio devoted to bespoke pieces. We believe every celebration deserves a treat that’s entirely its own.

We bake in small batches, design for each client individually, and obsess over the smallest details — from a colour gradient to the tiniest sugar flower.

The values behind every bake

a

Handcrafted

Every piece is made and finished by hand — no shortcuts, no mass production.

b

Bespoke

Designed around your story, colours and taste — truly one of a kind.

c

Real ingredients

Real butter, natural vanilla and fresh flavours — taste matters as much as looks.
